WristReader

An EPUB reader for Apple Watch. Bring your own books; no account needed.

What it does

  • Import .epub books on your iPhone (Files, share sheet or the import button); they are sent to your watch, where you read them, offline.
  • Adjustable typography (typeface, size, spacing, themes) from the watch or the iPhone.
  • Word lookup on the watch using online dictionaries (Wiktionary, and dictionaryapi.dev for English).
  • Optional: sync reading progress using the KOReader Sync protocol, so you can continue in apps that speak the same protocol, such as KOReader and Readest. Sync is off unless you connect a server you choose; the app does not provide one.
  • VoiceOver, larger text sizes and Dark Mode are supported.
  • No DRM-protected books. No accounts with the developer, no ads, no analytics.
