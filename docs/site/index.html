WristReader

An EPUB reader for Apple Watch that keeps your place in sync with the reading apps you already use.

What it does

  • Import .epub books on your iPhone (Files, share sheet or the import button); they are sent to your watch, where you read them.
  • Syncs reading progress using the KOReader Sync protocol, so progress can be shared with apps that speak the same protocol, such as KOReader and Readest. You need an account on a KOReader Sync server; the app does not provide one.
  • Adjustable typography (typeface, size, spacing, themes) from the watch or the iPhone.
  • Word lookup on the watch using online dictionaries (Wiktionary, and dictionaryapi.dev for English).
  • No DRM-protected books. No accounts with the developer, no ads, no analytics.
