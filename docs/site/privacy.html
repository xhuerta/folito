Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]. Applies to the WristReader apps for iPhone and Apple Watch.

In short: WristReader has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer runs no server that receives your data. The app only talks to the sync server you configure and, when you look up a word, to the online dictionaries named below.

Who is responsible

[DEVELOPER_NAME], contact: [CONTACT_EMAIL].

What stays on your devices

  • Your books (EPUB files you import) are copied into the app's storage on your iPhone and sent to your Apple Watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity. They are never uploaded by the app.
  • Your library, reading positions, reading and typography settings are stored on the device(s).
  • Your sync credentials (server address, username, password) are stored in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone and, because the watch syncs on its own, are also sent to your paired watch and stored in the watch's Keychain. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone other than the sync server you entered.
  • Deleting the app removes this data from that device.

Reading-progress sync (your KOReader Sync server)

If you enter a server and account, the app sends requests only to that server (by default the field is pre-filled with https://sync.koreader.rocks, the public KOReader server; you can change it to your own). Each request can include:

  • your username and an MD5 hash of your password (this is how the KOReader Sync protocol authenticates; the plain password is not sent);
  • a book identifier, which is a hash computed from the content of the EPUB file (not the title or the text);
  • your reading position in that book (a position reference and a percentage), a timestamp assigned by the server, the device name "WristReader" and a random identifier generated by the app on each install;
  • when you tap "Create account", your username and password hash to create the account on that server.

As with any internet request, the server sees your IP address. That server is run by whoever operates it, not by the developer; its own privacy policy and retention rules apply, and the app cannot delete data stored there (ask the server operator). If you use http:// (allowed only for local-network addresses) the traffic is not encrypted. If you do not configure a server, nothing is synced.

Dictionary lookups

When you ask to look up a word on the watch, the word you selected (and, if no exact entry exists, simple variants such as its lower-case or de-inflected form) is sent to en.wiktionary.org (Wikimedia Foundation) and, for English words, may also be sent to api.dictionaryapi.dev. The requests are HTTPS; each includes the word in the web address and a User-Agent naming the app and its public project page; those services see your IP address and handle it under their own policies (Wikimedia privacy policy). No other text from your book is sent. Looked-up definitions are cached on the watch (you can clear this in the watch settings).

What we do not do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No accounts with the developer; the developer receives no personal data from the app.
  • No sale or sharing of data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices and rights

You can remove your credentials in the app settings or delete the app at any time. For data held by a sync server or dictionary service, contact that service. For questions about this policy, write to [CONTACT_EMAIL].
