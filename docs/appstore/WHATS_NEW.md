# What's New

The "What's New in This Version" text for each release, in the eight languages of the listing, ready to paste into App Store Connect (the version page, field "What's New in This Version"). Apple requires it for every version after 1.0 (see `LISTING.md`).

The rules of `LISTING.md` apply here too. In particular:

* **Only what the version adds or fixes**, and only what a user can see. Features behind a flag that is off (auto-scroll in 1.1) are not mentioned.
* **No third-party names.** KOReader and Readest appear only in the description, next to the "not affiliated" sentence (guideline 5.2, see `REVIEW_RISKS.md`). Say "sync" or "your sync service" instead.
* **Limit**: 4000 characters per language.

## 1.1.0 (build 52)

### en-US

What's new in Folito 1.1:
• Bookmarks on Apple Watch: save your place and jump back anytime.
• Footnotes open right where you are, without losing your page.
• Mark books as finished.
• A new chapter list screen.
• Smoother reading: chapter jumps land on the heading, changing the text size keeps your place, and you can scroll seamlessly back and forth between chapters.
• Clearer sync: choose which books sync; books that can't sync stay on your watch.
• The iPhone shows real progress while sending a book to your watch.
• Plus many small fixes.

### es-ES

Novedades en Folito 1.1:
• Marcadores en el Apple Watch: guarda tu sitio y vuelve a él cuando quieras.
• Las notas al pie se abren ahí mismo, sin perder la página.

• Sincronización más clara: elige qué libros se sincronizan; los que no pueden sincronizarse se quedan en tu reloj.
• El iPhone muestra el progreso real al enviar un libro al reloj.
• Y muchos pequeños arreglos.
